Peer-Reviewed Journal Articles

Tomato Disease Detection: A Comparative Study of Transfer Learning with InceptionV3, DenseNet, and VGG19
Hassan Shoayb, Alina Banerjee
International Journal of Scientific Research and Technology (IJSART), Volume 11, Issue 12, December 2025. ISSN: 2395-1052.
[PDF] [Code] [Live Demo]
[Abstract]
This review and experimental investigation provides a rigorous comparative evaluation of transfer learning paradigms applied to agricultural plant pathology. Utilizing pre-trained Convolutional Neural Network architectures—specifically InceptionV3, DenseNet, and VGG19—the study benchmarks classification accuracy, training convergence, loss dynamics, and parameter efficiency on multi-class tomato leaf disease datasets. Experimental results demonstrate that InceptionV3 achieves optimal convergence and generalizability, delivering over 97% validation accuracy while maintaining low computational overhead suitable for browser-based edge deployment.

Applied Research & Engineering Systems

FactoryEye: A Real-Time AI Defect Detection System for Industrial Manufacturing
Hassan Shaibu
Technical Report & Production Implementation · Computer Vision & MLOps
[Repository]
[Overview]
FactoryEye is a production-grade automated visual inspection system designed for manufacturing lines. Utilizing fine-tuned YOLO architectures optimized for high-throughput video processing, the system identifies, bounds, and classifies surface anomalies and micro-defects on steel surfaces. Includes integrated experiment tracking, latency-minimized inference pipelines, and automated incident alert dispatching.
