Teaching Philosophy

My instruction centers on three foundational principles:

  • Demystifying Complexity: Complex algorithmic concepts in machine learning, deep learning, and calculus are grounded in intuitive analogies and direct visual intuition.
  • Theory Linked to Code: Every theoretical concept is coupled immediately with Python implementation from first principles before transitioning to industry frameworks (NumPy, PyTorch, Scikit-Learn).
  • Production Relevance: Curricula emphasize real-world workflows, debugging techniques, version control, and cloud deployment rather than purely synthetic textbook problems.
