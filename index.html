Research Publications

Tomato Disease Detection: A Comparative Study of Transfer Learning with InceptionV3, DenseNet, and VGG19
Hassan Shoayb, Alina Banerjee
International Journal of Scientific Research and Technology (IJSART), Volume 11, Issue 12, December 2025. ISSN: 2395-1052.
[PDF] [Code] [Live Demo]
[Abstract]
This study presents a comparative evaluation of pre-trained convolutional neural network architectures—specifically InceptionV3, DenseNet, and VGG19—for tomato leaf disease classification using transfer learning. The experimental evaluation analyzes convergence rates, validation accuracy, and inference latency under fine-tuned training regimes, demonstrating model generalization exceeding 97% validation accuracy for precision agricultural disease diagnostics.
